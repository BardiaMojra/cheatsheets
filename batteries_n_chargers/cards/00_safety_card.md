# LiPo Safety — Charging Table

!!! danger "Emergency — in this order"
    1. **Unplug the charger from the wall.** Not the battery first.
    2. Move the pack to the floor or the metal bin, clear of people and anything flammable.
    3. **Call Steve (lab manager) or a TA.** Do not leave it alone.
    4. If it is burning: get out, pull the alarm. **Do not use water on the pack.**

## The five rules

1. **Never leave a charging battery unattended.** Stay in the room, in sight of it.
2. **Charge in the LiPo bag or metal bin**, on the charging table — never on paper, foam,
   carpet or a laptop.
3. **Never charge a swollen, punctured, wet or hot pack.** Quarantine it.
4. **Check chemistry and cell count before you press start.** 3S charged as 6S is the
   most common way a pack catches fire.
5. **If in doubt, ask.** Nobody is annoyed by the question.

## Stop and quarantine if

| Situation | Do this |
|---|---|
| Pack is **swollen or puffed** | Do not charge. Metal bin. Tell Steve |
| Any cell **below 3.3 V** | Do not charge on normal settings. Tell a TA |
| Pack is **hot to the touch** | Let it cool fully before anything else |
| Pack was **crashed or crushed** | Watch it 30 min in the bin before storing |
| Pack **leaks or smells sharp/sweet** | Ventilate, quarantine, tell Steve. Do not inhale |
| Charger shows the **wrong cell count** | Stop. Do not override. Tell a TA |
| Pack is **dead / finished** | **Never in the trash.** Hazardous waste — ask Steve |

## Voltage limits — per cell, and by pack

| | per cell | 3S | 4S | 6S |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Full** | 4.20 V | 12.6 V | 16.8 V | 25.2 V |
| **Storage (shelf)** | 3.80 V | 11.4 V | 15.2 V | 22.8 V |
| **Land now** | 3.50 V | 10.5 V | 14.0 V | 21.0 V |
| **Stop** — working floor *(3.00 V = damage)* | **3.30 V** | 9.9 V | 13.2 V | 19.8 V |

!!! warning "Sitting more than ~2 days? Storage charge it — 3.80–3.85 V per cell."
    A pack left full ages fast and puffs on the rack. Storage mode is **not** disposal.
